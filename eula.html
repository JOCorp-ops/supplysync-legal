1. The service

SupplySync is a B2B inventory forecasting subscription service. It helps you calculate reorder points, days of supply, overstock, and reorder alerts.

SupplySync can connect to your QuickBooks Online company through Intuit’s OAuth process with read-only access. In that mode, it reads items, sales, purchases, and vendor data. It never writes to, changes, or deletes QuickBooks data.

SupplySync can also accept uploaded QuickBooks Desktop export files and optional shelf-count files.

The product does not use artificial intelligence to generate its results.
