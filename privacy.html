Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Andrew Jones, doing business as SupplySync (“SupplySync,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), located in Pennsylvania, USA, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the SupplySync inventory forecasting service. For questions, contact us at supplysync.support@gmail.com.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to business customers and their authorized users of SupplySync. SupplySync is a B2B service. We do not knowingly market to or collect personal information from children.

2. Information we collect

We collect the information needed to provide the service:

  • Account information, such as your name, business name, email address, and subscription or billing details.
  • QuickBooks Online data, when you connect your company file through Intuit OAuth with read-only access. This may include items, sales, purchases, and vendor data.
  • Uploaded files, if you provide QuickBooks Desktop export files or optional shelf-count files.
  • Technical information that is ordinarily generated when you use a hosted service, such as log data needed to operate and secure the service. We do not log OAuth tokens.

We do not collect more QuickBooks data than the service needs to calculate reorder points, days of supply, overstock, and reorder alerts.

3. How we use information

We use the information above solely to:

  • provide, maintain, and support the SupplySync service;
  • calculate inventory forecasts and alerts for your business;
  • manage your account and subscription; and
  • communicate with you about the service, security, and changes to our terms or this policy.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell customer data. We do not share customer data with third parties for their own marketing. We do not use customer data for advertising. We do not use customer data to train artificial intelligence models. The SupplySync product itself does not use AI.

5. How we connect to QuickBooks

When you connect QuickBooks Online, Intuit’s OAuth process asks you to grant SupplySync read-only access. SupplySync reads items, sales, purchases, and vendor data. It never writes to, changes, or deletes QuickBooks data.

OAuth tokens are stored securely and are never logged. You may disconnect SupplySync at any time from QuickBooks or from the app. Disconnecting revokes our access to your QuickBooks Online company.

6. Hosting and service providers

SupplySync is hosted by a third-party cloud hosting provider. We may also use a limited number of service providers that help us operate the service, such as payment or email providers, and only as needed to provide SupplySync. Those providers are permitted to process data only on our instructions and not for their own purposes.

QuickBooks Online is operated by Intuit. Intuit’s handling of your QuickBooks data is governed by Intuit’s own privacy policy and terms.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service and meet legal or accounting obligations.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing supplysync.support@gmail.com. We will also delete your customer data within 30 days after cancellation of your subscription, except for information we are required by law to keep (for example, limited billing records).

8. Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect the information we hold, including secure storage of OAuth tokens and a practice of never logging those tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You may:

  • disconnect QuickBooks access at any time, which revokes our access;
  • request a copy or correction of the account information we hold;
  • request deletion of your data, as described above; and
  • cancel your subscription and stop using the service.

Depending on where you or your business are located, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law. To exercise any of these rights, email supplysync.support@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will post the updated policy, update the effective date, and may also notify you by email or in the product. The revised policy applies from the effective date shown at the top of the page.
